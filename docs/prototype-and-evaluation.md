# Prototype and heuristic evaluation

## Three interactions to demonstrate on November 17

1. Search and filter: A student looks for a quiet spot with outlets, then compares the results in the map and list.
2. Location details: The student opens a spot and checks noise, seating, Wi-Fi, and other students' reviews.
3. Submit a review: A signed-in student rates a spot, writes a review, and sees it appear on the detail view.

For each interaction, show desktop and mobile layouts and make every step clickable in the digital prototype. Prepare a short task prompt for the other team to follow with mouse control.
