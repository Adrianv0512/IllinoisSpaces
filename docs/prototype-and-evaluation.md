# Prototype and heuristic evaluation

## Three interactions to demonstrate on November 17, 2026

1. Search and filter: A student looks for a quiet spot with outlets, then compares the results in the map and list.
2. Location details: The student opens a spot and checks noise, seating, Wi-Fi, and other students' reviews.
3. Submit a review: A signed-in student rates a spot, writes a review, and sees it appear on the detail view.

## Evaluator task prompts

Give the other team mouse control and read each task without describing where to click.

1. **Find a place:** "You need a quiet study space with plenty of outlets. Find one that looks promising." Start at the landing page. Observe whether the evaluator finds the search, filters, and map/list controls.
2. **Check the details:** "Before going there, find out whether the space has strong Wi-Fi and what students say about it." Observe how they open a location and scan the detail view.
3. **Share an experience:** "You visited this space yesterday. Give it five stars and write a short review." The evaluator can use **Demo sign in** with any display name. Observe whether they understand the sign-in step, rating control, and posting confirmation.

After the three tasks, ask the evaluator to use a narrow browser window and switch between the list and map. Note any control that becomes hard to find or use.

## Prototype boundaries

All study spot details, ratings, reviews, and map positions are illustrative. Demo sign in is simulated, and submitted reviews stay in that browser's local storage. The map is a conceptual campus view, not a navigation tool. Explain these limits before the evaluation so feedback stays focused on the interface.

## Heuristic prompts

- Is it clear which filters are active and how to reset them?
- Does clicking a map pin give enough feedback before opening details?
- Can the evaluator tell whether a review was posted and find it afterward?
- Are labels, contrast, spacing, and touch targets usable at a narrow width?
- Does the demo sign-in step make sense in the review flow?
